# Good words.<br>A clearer view.

Your notes, documentation, and next big idea — with a little more room to breathe. Welcome to **ClearMark**, a quiet home for your Markdown.

> [!TIP]
> Open a document with **⌘O**, or drop one anywhere in this window. Keep writing in your favorite editor; the preview follows along.

## The details make the difference

Good tools get out of your way. ClearMark gives your words considered typography, your documents a useful outline, and your eyes a choice of light, dark, or warm paper.

| A small thing | A better reading experience |
| :--- | :--- |
| **An outline that follows the structure** | Jump straight to the part you need. |
| **A view for every moment** | Read the result, inspect the source, or see both. |
| **Everything stays on your Mac** | No accounts, tracking, or network requests. |

## Code, in good company

Syntax highlighting makes the important parts stand out. One click copies a code block, ready for wherever your work takes you.

```swift
struct AGoodTool {
    let name = "ClearMark"
    let price = 0

    func open(_ idea: Markdown) -> Clarity {
        // Less friction. More room to think.
        return idea.beautifullyRendered()
    }
}
```

## A little space for the complex

From a quick inline expression like $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ to something that deserves its own line, math is part of the page.

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$
